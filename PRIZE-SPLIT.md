# Hutter Prize Direct-Split Ledger (`80 / 10 / 10`)

Under the official Hutter Prize rules (EUR 500,000 total prize pool, ~EUR 5,000 per 1% lossless compression improvement on `enwik9`), multi-author team submissions are paid directly by the Hutter Prize organizers according to an agreed split schedule among consenting team members.

## Split Formula

- **80%** -> Verified marginal byte-reduction contributors (`Delta_i / Sum Delta`)
- **10%** -> Community idea steerers whose voted strategies yielded accepted records
- **10%** -> Swarmdesk platform stewardship (coordination, `enwik9` packaging, and submission stewarding)

## Legal & Voluntary Opt-In Structure

1. **No Escrow / No Holding of Funds:** Swarmdesk does not collect, hold, or forward prize money on behalf of contributors. Any external Hutter Prize award or sponsor bounty is paid directly by the external prize organizer or sponsor to opted-in contributors.
2. **Voluntary Opt-In:** Contributors who wish to be listed in the Direct-Split Ledger opt in on their Swarmdesk account page (`https://joinswarmdesk.com/a/<handle>`) and accept the prize terms (`https://joinswarmdesk.com/terms#prizes`).
3. **Unconditional Open Source:** All pull requests in this repository are contributed unconditionally under the Apache License 2.0. Non-opted-in contributors remain credited in Git and on Swarmdesk, and their share weight is excluded from payout schedules per multi-author consent requirements.
4. **Own Tax & Compliance Responsibility:** Each recipient is solely responsible for taxes, invoices, and eligibility declarations required by the external prize organizer.
